## About

I am an M.S. researcher in Geological Sciences at IISER Kolkata, building on a B.Sc. (Honours) in Geology with minors in Physics and Mathematics. My work combines field-based geological interpretation with seismic methods, well-log analysis, and machine learning, with a long-term interest in critical-mineral exploration and resource characterisation. My current research applies seismic tomography and receiver-function analysis to image subsurface volcanic structures, building directly on earlier research in ML-based lithology prediction (CSIR-NGRI) and gas-hydrate exploration (National Gas Hydrate Project). I am interested in developing quantitative, reproducible methods for subsurface characterisation and exploration decision-making under geological uncertainty.

## Current Research

**Seismic Imaging of Subsurface Volcanic Structures** — *IISER Kolkata, Ongoing*

Independent M.S. research project applying seismic tomography and receiver-function methods to characterise subsurface volcanic structures. The work spans the full pipeline: teleseismic data retrieval, quality control, deconvolution and stacking, and processing of multi-station datasets to resolve subsurface velocity contrasts relevant to volcanic plumbing systems, with results integrated against geological constraints. The project is in active development; no final structural results are being claimed at this stage.

## Research Experience

**CSIR-NGRI — Advanced Lithology Prediction Using Machine Learning** (*May–Jul 2024*)
Designed and implemented machine-learning models to classify subsurface lithology from well-log data, and benchmarked the predictions against conventional lithological interpretation. Presented at CSIR-NGRI and recognised with the Top 10 Summer Thesis Award (national level, ~5,000 participants).

**CSIR-NGRI — Gas Hydrate Exploration** (*National Gas Hydrate Project, 2024*)
Evaluated gas-hydrate potential along the Indian coastal belt and investigated the geological and geophysical controls on hydrate occurrence and stability, in the context of unconventional energy-resource assessment.

**IMD — Heatwave Dynamics & Short-Term Weather Prediction** (*May–Jun 2025*)
Analysed the spatial and temporal evolution of heatwave events over the Delhi region and characterised short-duration, high-intensity rainfall bursts, working with large meteorological time-series datasets — a skill set directly transferable to time-series-heavy geophysical signal processing.
