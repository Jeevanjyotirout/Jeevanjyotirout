## About

I work at the interface of geology, geophysics and geochemistry, using numerical modelling and machine learning to study subsurface processes. My experience spans ore-deposit exploration (chromite drill-core logging at OMC, Odisha), machine-learning lithology prediction from well logs (CSIR-NGRI), reactive-transport modelling in PFLOTRAN, and seismic tomography.

I combine field observation, laboratory analysis and quantitative methods to understand Earth processes, natural resources and sustainable energy.
